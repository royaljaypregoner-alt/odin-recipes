Mango float

Description

Mango Float is a classic, beloved Filipino icebox cake dessert. It is made with layers of sweet ripe mangoes, graham crackers, and a rich whipped cream mixture made from condensed milk and heavy cream.

This dessert requires absolutely no baking, making it an incredibly popular treat to make for holidays, special occasions, or a hot summer day. It is traditionally chilled or frozen overnight before serving.

Ingredients

  • 3 to 4 large ripe mangoes (sliced into thin strips)
  • 2 cups heavy whipping cream (chilled)
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 box of whole graham crackers
  • Optional: Crushed graham crackers for the top garnish

    • Steps

    1. Prepare the Fruit: Peel your ripe mangoes, slice the flesh off the pits, and slice into thin even strips. Set them aside.
    2. Whip the Cream: In a large chilled mixing bowl, whip the heavy cream with a hand mixer until smooth and thick. Slowly fold in the sweetened condensed milk until well combined.
    3. Build the Base Layer: Arrange a single flat layer of whole graham crackers across the bottom of an 8x8 or 9x13 glass baking dish. Break pieces to fit any gaps cleanly.
    4. Layer the Cream and Fruit: Spread a thick, generous layer of your whipped cream mixture smoothly over the graham base. Arrange an even layer of your sliced mangoes right on top of the cream.
    5. Repeat the Process: Add another layer of graham crackers, followed by a layer of cream and a layer of mangoes. Repeat this step until your ingredients are used up, ending with a cream layer on top.
    6. Garnish and Finish: Decorate the top with your prettiest remaining mango slices and sprinkle a layer of crushed graham crackers over the surface.
    7. Chill and Serve: Cover the dish with plastic wrap and freeze or refrigerate for at least 6 hours (or overnight) so the crackers soften into a cake-like texture. Slice and enjoy cold!
