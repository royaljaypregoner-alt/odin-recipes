Fried Chicken

Description

This recipe makes perfectly crispy, golden-brown fried chicken that stays incredibly juicy on the inside. The secret is letting the chicken marinate and using a well-seasoned flour dredging.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces (thighs, drumsticks, or wings)
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup buttermilk (or milk mixed with a splash of vinegar)
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 1 tablespoon paprika
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Oil for deep frying

    • Steps

    1. Marinate: Soak the chicken pieces in buttermilk seasoned with a bit of salt and pepper for at least 30 minutes in the fridge.
    2. Prepare the Coating: In a large shallow bowl, thoroughly mix the flour, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper.
    3. Dredge the Chicken: Take a piece of chicken from the milk, let the excess drip off, and roll it generously in the seasoned flour mixture until fully covered.
    4. Heat the Oil: Pour oil into a deep pan or pot and heat it up to around 350°F (175°C).
    5. Fry to Golden Perfection: Carefully lower the chicken pieces into the hot oil. Fry for about 12 to 15 minutes, turning occasionally, until the skin turns crunchy and deep golden brown.
    6. Drain and Serve: Place the cooked chicken on a wire rack or paper towels to drain excess oil, then serve hot!
