Filipino Pork Humba

Description

Humba is a classic Visayan braised pork belly dish. It shares similarities with Pork Adobo, but sets itself apart with its distinctly rich, sweet, and savory profile enhanced by brown sugar, fermented black beans, and banana blossoms.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly (cut into bite-sized cubes)
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup vinegar
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 3 tablespoons salted black beans (tausi)
  • 1/4 cup dried banana blossoms
  • 5 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 2 pieces dried bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole peppercorns

Steps

  1. Sear the Pork: Heat a deep pan over medium-high heat. Add the pork belly pieces and cook until they brown slightly and release their natural oils. Drain any excessive fat if needed.
  2. Sauté Aromatics: Push the meat to one side of the pan. Add the minced garlic and sauté until fragrant.
  3. Simmer with Liquids: Pour in the soy sauce, vinegar, and water (enough to submerge the meat). Bring the liquid to a rolling boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and let it simmer for about 40 minutes until the pork gets tender.
  4. Add Flavors: Stir in the brown sugar, whole peppercorns, dried bay leaves, salted black beans, and dried banana blossoms.
  5. Reduce the Sauce: Continue cooking uncovered on low heat until the sauce thickens up into a glossy, sticky glaze and the pork belly becomes melt-in-your-mouth tender.
  6. Serve: Turn off the heat and transfer your hot humba to a serving bowl. Pairs perfectly with warm cups of steamed white rice!
